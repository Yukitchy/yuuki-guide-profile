Yūki is a Tokyo-based Akihabara tour guide. Yūki has loved anime, manga and games since before starting school, has been coming to Akihabara since high school, and has watched more than 1,500 anime series.

Since 2019, Yūki has guided visitors from abroad through Akihabara, mostly from the US, the UK, Canada and Australia. On Airbnb the tour holds a 4.96 rating from 171 reviews, and on Tripadvisor and Viator it has more than 170 reviews, all five stars.

There is no agency in between. Yūki scouts the shops and runs every tour in person. Before each tour, guests share the series they love, and the route is planned around that list. Many families write that the kids and the parents both had a great time. The tour is covered by liability insurance.

Yūki also teaches Japanese online and has given more than 2,100 lessons on italki. In September 2026, Business Insider Japan featured the work as an Akihabara tour guide.
